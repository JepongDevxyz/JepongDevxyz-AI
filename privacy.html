Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how JepongDevxyz AI handles information when you use chat, files, account sync, AI providers, web tools, and connected plugins.

1. Information you choose to provide

  • Prompts, chat messages, files, images, video frames, links, and tool requests.
  • Optional profile information and preferences you save in the app.
  • Optional API keys or connection details you add for external providers.

2. Local app data

JepongDevxyz AI uses browser/device storage for settings and local chat features. Clearing site/app data can remove locally stored information. Some features also use account-based synchronization when you choose to sign in.

3. AI providers and tools

To generate a response or use a tool, JepongDevxyz AI may send the relevant prompt, conversation context, attachment data, or extracted attachment content to the provider or service selected for that request — for example xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, depending on the model you choose. Data handling by those third parties is governed by their own policies.

4. Account authentication

If you sign in using Google, Facebook, GitHub, email, or another supported identity provider, authentication is handled through the configured sign-in service. JepongDevxyz AI does not ask you to enter a third-party provider password directly into its own chat interface.

5. Connected services and plugins

If you connect a service (such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Spotify, GitHub, or Vercel) or install a plugin, the app may access only the data and actions needed for the features you invoke, subject to the permissions granted to that integration. Connection tokens are stored securely and only used when you request an action.

6. Credits and payments

New accounts receive 500 free welcome credits. Usage and credit-balance counters are stored so billing stays accurate. Credit top-ups are processed by PayMongo — we do not store your card numbers or bank details.

7. Security and credentials

Use unique, revocable API keys where possible. Do not put secrets in ordinary chat messages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so review the permissions and account controls of every provider you connect.

8. Your controls

You can manage local chats, settings, connected services, and supported account features from the app. Disconnect any service anytime in Settings → Connectors to revoke access and delete its tokens, or permanently delete your account and associated data in Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also clear browser/app storage to remove locally stored data from that device.

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Policy updates

This Policy may change as JepongDevxyz AI adds or removes providers, storage features, plugins, or account capabilities. The app can request renewed acceptance when a materially updated policy version is introduced.

Privacy-sensitive behavior depends partly on the external providers you choose. Review each provider's policy before sending sensitive or confidential information.
