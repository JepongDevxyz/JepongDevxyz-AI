Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

JepongDevxyz AI ("we", "us") is an AI chat assistant that lets you converse with multiple AI models — including xAI Grok, OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini — connect your favorite services, and manage everything from one place. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in (via email one-time code or OAuth), we receive your email address and basic profile information through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Chat content. The messages you send, plus any files or images you attach, are processed to generate AI responses. Your chat history is stored so you can revisit past conversations on any device.
  • Connected services. If you connect a third-party service (for example Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Spotify, GitHub, or Vercel), we store the OAuth tokens needed to act on your behalf. We only request the permissions shown on the provider's consent screen (for example, read-only access to email), and we only access your data when you ask us to.
  • Usage and credits. New accounts receive 500 free welcome credits. We keep counters of your feature usage, credit balance, and top-up history so billing stays accurate.
  • Payment information. Credit top-ups are processed by PayMongo. We do not store your card numbers or bank details.
  • Technical information. Basic device and diagnostic data (such as app version and error reports) used to keep the service reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate the service: generating AI responses, running connector actions you request, and managing your credits and top-ups.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the service and fix bugs.
  • To communicate with you about important changes.

3. How we share your information

  • AI model providers. Your messages are sent to the provider of the model you choose (xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or others) so it can generate a response. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.
  • Connected services. When you connect a service, your requests are carried out through that provider under its terms (for example, Google's policies apply to Gmail, Drive, and Calendar data).
  • Infrastructure providers. We use Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (hosting), and PayMongo (payments) to run the service.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.
We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect anytime. Remove any connected service in Settings → Connectors. This revokes our access and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Delete your account. Permanently delete your account and associated data in Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Access and correction. Email us and we will help you review or correct your information.

5. Data security and retention

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls on our database. We keep your information only while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Children

JepongDevxyz AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the app. Continued use of the service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
